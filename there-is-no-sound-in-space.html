There Is No Sound in Space

An exploration of the unknown through AI image-making and cinematic memory.

Inspired by the vast and unreachable nature of space, the project experiments with building unfamiliar environments through a retro-futuristic visual language.

Film grain, aged textures and atmospheric lighting transform imagined worlds into fragments of a science-fiction film that never existed.
