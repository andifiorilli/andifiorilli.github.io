Owl Paper

Owl Paper is a surreal visual series conceived as a collection of contemporary ornamental landscapes.

Animals, vegetation, geometric structures and decorative patterns combine to create imaginary scenes that sit somewhere between wallpaper, printed tapestry and fictional natural history.

Organized as four evolving sequences, the project explores how one composition can transform into another while preserving a shared visual language.
